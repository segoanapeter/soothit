Before you hand over your technology

Questions you should ask.

Trust matters when someone is helping with your personal technology.

Can you help me remotely?

Many software, email, configuration and troubleshooting problems can be diagnosed or resolved remotely. If the problem requires physical repair, we'll tell you.

Do I need to understand computers?

No. Explain what you're seeing or experiencing in your own words. Figuring out the technical part is our job.

Is remote support safe?

Remote access should always happen with your knowledge and consent. We will build Sooth's support process around transparent and controlled access.

What if you can't fix my problem?

We should be clear about what can be fixed remotely and when a device needs physical inspection, repair or specialist assistance.
